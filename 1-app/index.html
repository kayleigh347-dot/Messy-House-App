Help

Find your way around

House opens your rooms. Doing Now holds the tasks you are working on. More opens Manage home, task lists and Backup & custom tasks. Task Data keeps completion history.

Tasks and subtasks

Tap a task name to expand its steps. Swipe right to complete. Use Search & filters to find overdue, today's, upcoming or undated tasks. Reset clears those filters.

Mess level

This measures unfinished cleaning work, not completed work. Higher means more mess. Task impact and overdue work affect it. Sidequests usually do not add mess.

Points and Undo

Sign in to record who completed a task and award points. Completing subtasks awards their points; their parent adds no extra point. Undo reverses a completion. Task lists show today's completions; Task Data keeps older history.

Recurring and reusable tasks

Recurring tasks have a schedule. Completing one creates its next occurrence. Edit its next due date or pause it in recurring options. Reusable tasks are saved templates you can add when needed. Laundry cycles have their own tab.

Backups and recovery

Export saves a copy. Import previews a merge: backup records replace matching IDs, while other existing records stay. A recovery copy is saved before import. Storage warnings mean changes may not survive closing the app; export them before leaving.

Account and sharing

Local tasks stay on this browser. Sign in on this device to sync and record household points. Use the same house on your devices. Offline changes sync when you reconnect.

Planning a manageable task

Use More → Manage home → Tasks → Tools/times to measure a task. Recorded times change estimates only when you choose Use as estimate. Task times are automatic. Change a base estimate only in More → Manage home → Tasks → Tools/times. Snooze moves the current task to tomorrow or a chosen date, keeping its repeat pattern. Select existing tasks in a parent’s subtasks to move several tasks under it.

Phone timers

Set up Automate for Android, or House Timer in iPhone Shortcuts, in Settings. Set timer sends the duration to your phone Clock. Check your phone: the app cannot confirm alarm delivery. Stop a phone timer in Clock. iPhone alarm delivery has not yet been tested.

Calendar and notifications

Calendar shows scheduled tasks, appointments and errands. Change a task's due date through its date control, or move its room on the calendar. Enable notifications in Account & sync on each device.

Account & sync

Create and organise tasks locally. Sign in on this device to record completions, earn points and sync your household.

In-app task alerts work automatically. Device alerts are optional.

Share my house

Each person signs in with their own email. Members can add, edit and complete tasks in the shared house.

Current house: My house

Invites are single-use and expire after 7 days. Send the code yourself; no email is sent automatically.

People with access to my house

House sharing needs its database update. Download the sharing update and run it in your existing Supabase project’s SQL Editor, then try again.
