Help

A simple way to start
  1. Open House and choose a room.
  2. For a guided session, choose Focus on this room beside its name. Clear the Way opens first. You can choose preparation steps or select Not Now.
  3. Choose the room tasks you want to work on, then Start focus. You can also select a task yourself using I'm on it in detailed view.
  4. Open Doing Now to see the work you selected. Complete tasks there or in the room; they are the same tasks.
  5. Use Undo if you completed something by mistake. Sign in on this device to record completions and points.

You do not have to follow a calendar recommendation to use the app. To choose flexible recommendations instead of a routine, use More → Manage home → Schedule → Go With the Flow.

Find your way around
  • House: your rooms, their tasks, room scenes and mess levels.
  • Doing Now: tasks you have chosen to work on, with Needs done and Laundry tabs.
  • Side quests: other projects and activities. A room's side quests are separate from its cleaning tasks.
  • Task Data: recorded completions and activity information.
  • Calendar: routine planning or flexible recommendations, depending on your personal mode, alongside appointments and errands.
  • More: Manage home, Active tasks, laundry, Backup & custom tasks, Settings, Mystery Shelf, Household organiser and House notes.

Manage home has Tasks, Schedule and My rooms tabs. Tasks contains Reusable, Recurring and Tools/times. Schedule contains Manual edit and Schedule maker. Laundry setup and editing have their own entry in More.

Open the settings cog or More → Settings for Settings, Account & sync and this Help tab.

Add, find and edit tasks

Choose Add task in a room, Doing Now or an ordinary task list. Enter a name and check the destination room. You can choose Add to Doing Now, Needs attention or Sidequest. Add subtasks for the smaller steps of a job; More options contains additional task settings.

Compact view keeps cards small: task name, subtask count, completion and First/Last ordering controls. Tap a task name to expand its subtasks. Switch to Detailed view for editing, notes, assignment, timers, Doing Now and other task options.

Use the magnifying glass to search within a room. If you have created personal areas, use All areas below the task toolbar to narrow the list. Other task pages have their own search/filter controls. Clearing a search or resetting filters changes what is shown; it does not delete tasks.

Complete a task with its completion control or by swiping right. Completing a parent can complete its unfinished steps. Completing the last unfinished step can finish the parent. Check the task and its steps before completing the whole job.

Doing Now and room Focus

Doing Now holds work you deliberately selected. In detailed task view, I'm on it adds that task; Not on it removes it from Doing Now without deleting it from the room.

Focus on this room opens Clear the Way first, then a separate choice of connected room tasks. In Go With the Flow, that view also shows your current recommendations and why they are suggested. Tick what you want and choose Start focus. Ordinary completion, Undo and timer actions still apply.

The Focus button in Doing Now lets you choose a room and opens the same sequence. Merely opening Focus or choosing a room adds no tasks and does not replace your existing Doing Now choices. Close Focus whenever you want; selected tasks remain available in their normal lists.

Choose more tasks returns to the selection view. You can work on your own choices even if no recommendations are available.

Clear the Way and sorting later

Clear the Way is preparation inside room Focus. The standard routine helps you collect items for other rooms, protect important items, collect obvious rubbish and clear loose floor clutter. Read each step's instructions; boxing clutter is separate from sorting it.

Choose a routine, then Add All or Choose Tasks. Review the proposed steps and room before confirming. Not Now, cancellation or simply viewing a routine adds nothing. Confirmed preparation tasks use the same task identities in the room and Doing Now.

For pickup/boxing steps, you can choose the whole room or separately named areas. After you complete a deliberately added pickup step, its sorting task is added to the room's ordinary list. Each chosen category/area has its own follow-up. Important-item sorting receives higher priority. Sorting does not have to be finished before cleaning and is not automatically selected in Doing Now.

Undoing pickup removes its automatic sorting task only if that task is untouched. Sorting you edited, reordered, started or completed is kept. Finishing an old preparation session does not prevent deliberately starting another one later.

Create and customise routines in Manage home → My rooms. New base routines are shared with the household. Editing a shared routine creates your personal variation; hiding it from your view does not remove another member's original.

Choose a cleaning mode

Find Go With the Flow in More → Manage home → Schedule. Checked means flexible recommendations; unchecked means Follow a Routine. Each member chooses their own mode. Changing yours does not change another member's mode or erase the household schedule.

Follow a Routine: use the recurring schedule and planning Calendar. Catch up helps you find missed recurring work.

Go With the Flow: Calendar recommends work without Catch up or backlog obligations. A suggestion does not automatically become a room task or Doing Now task. You choose what to add. Existing household tasks and schedules remain saved.

The first enable shows an explanation. It stays available in Help afterward. Sign in and connect to save your personal mode across your devices. A disabled checkbox or connection message means your personal settings are not currently ready to save.

Set up flexible recommendations
  1. Enable Go With the Flow in Manage home → Schedule.
  2. Expand Flexible recommendation settings. Check your time zone and preferred daily suggestion count/minutes.
  3. Open Task recommendation choices. For each task you want recommended, choose its inclusion, category and recommendation priority. Check its initial suggested date and, for a saved template, its destination room.
  4. Choose Days, Weeks, As needed or Use existing frequency / learn. Save each task's recommendation settings.
  5. Choose Finish setup when it appears. Setup saves recommendation choices; it adds no household tasks.

Priority 1 is most important and 5 least important. Recommend first (urgent task) gives a task precedence in your recommendations. These choices do not change the ordinary household task priority or its due dates.

The starting daily suggestion limits are 5 suggestions or 60 minutes. They are soft limits: other choices remain under More recommendations. Adjust them to suit you; they are not a required amount of cleaning.

Deep clean covers pairs a deeper clean with a routine task. It avoids recommending both together when the deep clean covers that work. Completing the deep clean can move the routine's next personal suggestion; it does not award a second completion or alter the household routine's due date.

Use the flexible Calendar

Use Week or Month, the arrows and Today to browse dates. In Week, Overview shows the week and Day detail returns to the selected day's recommendations. Tap a day to inspect it. Use the room filter for one room or household events.

Each suggestion explains why it is recommended. Choose Add to Doing Now when you want to work on it. An existing task is reused. For a recommendation that needs a new task, review the title, room and household visibility in the confirmation first; Cancel adds nothing.

Today's recommendation order is saved at 03:00 in your chosen time zone, or when you next reopen the app after that boundary. Completing work updates its status without reshuffling today's order. Changes to recommendation settings normally apply at the next daily refresh.

An unfinished flexible suggestion carries forward with its original suggested date. An event step with a target date keeps that date when missed. Flexible suggestions do not create a Catch up list.

To hide a suggestion just for yourself, open its Options → Stop recommending this task. Restore it in Flexible recommendation settings → Hidden recommendations. Hiding a recommendation does not delete or pause the shared task, change its due date or hide it for another member.

Learning how often you do a task

With Keep accurate completion dates for learning enabled, the app keeps separate completion dates for eligible saved tasks. After at least three genuine completions, it can use the average gap between completions to suggest an interval. Later completions update that average. The starting choice rounds learned intervals up to whole days.

A frequency you explicitly choose takes precedence over a learned interval. As needed means no automatic suggested due date. Cleaning, maintenance and pet-care work can be eligible; organisation needs explicit inclusion. Crafts/hobbies, subtasks, Clear the Way and event preparation do not teach cleaning intervals.

Learning uses recorded dates, not guessed historical dates. The same completion counts once; genuinely separate completions on the same day remain separate. Ordinary Undo corrects the learning record as well as the normal completion.

Delete my learning dates deletes only these separate personal dates after confirmation. It does not delete household tasks, normal completion history or points. Switching off collection does not itself erase dates already saved.

Events and preparation

Use Flexible recommendation settings → Events & preparation for optional Christmas, Birthday or custom events. Choose the actual event date, whether it repeats annually, and the preparation steps you want. Template steps are editable; saving an event creates recommendations, not ordinary tasks.

For each step, enter a lead time in Days or Weeks and choose a room. Four weeks means 28 calendar days before the event. Flexible suggestion steps can carry forward; Fixed target date steps stay anchored to their calculated date.

Choose whether unfinished preparation is kept or hidden after the event. For an annual 29 February occasion, choose the fallback date for other years. Each annual occurrence has its own completion, so completing this year's step does not complete next year's.

Event definitions and recommendation visibility are personal. Explicitly adding an event step creates a task visible in the shared destination room and Doing Now; check the addition preview, especially for gift-related work. Delete from my view removes your event definition from view, not tasks already added.

Personal room setups and areas

Open More → Manage home → My rooms → Personal room setups & areas. Set up one room or review your rooms. You can go Back, Skip a room, continue to the next room, or save and finish later.

Name and save a setup to keep its choices. Saving alone adds no tasks. Add all or Add selected is a separate action: review the destination and proposed additions before confirming. Existing referenced tasks are reused rather than duplicated or having their schedules replaced.

Areas are optional names for parts of a room, such as Fish Tank Area. Create them and assign tasks in My rooms. In the room, All areas below the toolbar switches the personal filter. It does not move tasks to a different room.

Setups, areas and assignments belong to you. Renaming or deleting an area changes your organisation, not another member's areas or the shared tasks. Tasks from a deleted area remain available as unassigned.

Routine Calendar, dates and Catch up

In Follow a Routine, Calendar shows recurring cleaning work, appointments and errands. Its cleaning checklists and whole-house choices contain recurring tasks; ordinary one-off work stays in task lists.

Due date is the task's scheduled date. A planned date is where you choose to place an occurrence on the planning Calendar. Moving a planned occurrence does not rewrite its due date or repeat rule. Projected repeats show future occurrences; they are not copies to complete in advance.

Tap a day for details. Use its Edit control to choose the task/session or move that planned day. Undo planned move reverses an eligible planned move. A Whole-house session groups chosen recurring tasks without completing them together automatically.

Skip this time skips one recurring occurrence without awarding points or recording a completion; later repeats stay scheduled. Catch up groups missed recurring work by task series rather than adding another overdue entry for every repeat.

Manage repeat rules in Manage home → Tasks → Recurring or Schedule → Manual edit. Pausing work is different from completing it. Use the appropriate task options rather than changing personal recommendation settings to alter a household schedule.

Schedule maker and saved reusable tasks

Manage home → Schedule → Schedule maker helps prepare a recurring cleaning schedule. You can choose rooms/tasks, intervals, tools, rest days, time limits and one room per day. Use current schedule preselects existing recurring choices. Review the visual preview and the routines being replaced before confirming.

Schedule maker can replace existing recurring cleaning routines after explicit confirmation, including routines in rooms you leave unchecked. Keep the offered local board backup if you want a recovery copy. Laundry and ordinary one-off tasks have separate workflows.

Manage home → Tasks → Reusable holds saved task definitions. Add task creates work from a saved definition; an available active task can be reused rather than added again. The displayed Remove action removes its active task family from Tasks and is a shared task action. Read the control carefully: removing actual work is different from hiding a saved definition or a personal recommendation.

Delete on a saved definition removes it only from your personal saved-task view after confirmation. Other members keep their copy, and existing tasks, laundry progress and history remain. Restore it from Hidden saved tasks in Reusable. You need connected personal settings to save or restore this choice.

Tools, task times and phone timers

Set a task's tools in its editing/options controls. Tools needed in Doing Now or a routine Calendar day opens a checklist with each required tool once, including tools for unfinished subtasks.

To measure a task, use More → Manage home → Tasks → Tools/times. Choose a task, Start timing, Pause/Resume as needed, then Stop and save time. You can also enter a measured time. Saving a measurement does not complete the task or automatically change its estimate; choose Use as estimate on the recorded time when you want that change.

Edit base time changes the selected task's base minutes. Preset time multipliers such as ×2/×3 affect preset portions; saved or measured times are kept as entered. An estimate helps planning but does not require you to finish within that time.

Set timer on a task opens the phone countdown controls. Set up the connection in Settings → Phone timer setup: Automate for Android, or the House Timer shortcut on iPhone. Connection settings stay on that device. Sending a request does not complete the task. Check your phone's Clock to confirm the timer started; stop or change it there. The app cannot confirm Android delivery, and iPhone delivery has not been verified.

Laundry

Open Doing Now → Laundry or More → Laundry to see active loads and other laundry tasks. Loads can overlap: one may be washing while another is on the hanging rail or waiting to be put away. Advance the steps on the correct load rather than starting over.

Use the dryer, hanging rail or both as appropriate. A put-away task is separate from washing/hanging. Uniform can have its own cycle and reminders. Completing a wash or hang step does not mean the entire load has been put away.

Use More → Laundry setup & editing to edit cycle steps and routes. Changing setup preserves active load progress. Use the ordinary completion and Undo controls for steps; review Undo if later steps are already complete.

Other laundry tasks can be added in the laundry panel. Cleaning room setup and Go With the Flow recommendations do not replace laundry's workflow.

Mess levels, points and completion history

The room's mess estimate reflects unfinished cleaning work and task impact. It is a guide, not a record of how the room looks in real life. Parent groups do not add another contribution on top of their leaf tasks. Due/overdue work can affect the estimate; completed work does not remain unfinished mess.

If the estimate does not match the room, open Adjust mess level, choose the level and Set mess level. Use task estimate returns to the task-based estimate. This changes the room's displayed estimate, not its tasks or completion history.

Sign in on this device to record who completed work and earn points. Subtasks receive their normal completion credit; completing a parent group does not add another reward for the same subtask work. Recommendations and learning dates do not award extra points.

Completed tasks are grouped below unfinished work, with Undo beside them. Undo reverses the relevant completion using the ordinary task rules. Task lists show recent/today's completed work; Task Data is where you inspect recorded activity beyond that view. Mystery Shelf contains rewards.

Accounts, household sharing and personal choices

Open Settings → Account & sync, enter your email and choose Email me a sign-in link. Follow that link to sign in on this device. Sign in separately on each device you want to use. Use your household name to identify your completions.

For a shared house, each member uses their own email. In Share my house, the owner creates an invite and sends the code to the other person. They enter the code and choose Join house. Invites are single-use and expire after seven days. Choose the intended house and Open selected house; refresh the list if needed.

Household tasks, edits, actual task deletion, completions and points are shared. Your planning mode, recommendation choices/suppression, learning dates, personal room setups/areas, routine variations and event definitions are personal. Newly created base Clear the Way routines are shared; your personal variation/hiding does not overwrite another member's original.

Offline changes normally wait to sync when the connection returns. Check save/connection messages rather than assuming an offline change reached the server. Personal settings may need a connection before saving; offline recommendations may use the last saved snapshot.

Backups, imports and recovery

Use More → Backup & custom tasks or the backup controls in Settings. Export board backup downloads a local copy of the household board. Keep it somewhere you can find again.

A board export covers shared board data. It does not include your separately stored personal Flow preferences, room setups/areas or learning dates, and it is not a copy of this device's phone-timer connection settings.

Import is deliberate: choose a backup, inspect the preview, then confirm only if you want its contents merged into the current board. Matching records can be replaced by records from the chosen backup; other current records are retained. This can affect shared household data. A pre-import recovery copy is kept locally when available.

Custom-task CSV upload is a separate action. Read its preview and choices before applying it. An app update does not require importing an old backup or replacing your board.

If you see a storage warning, export a board backup before closing the app. Do not clear browser/site data as a troubleshooting step; it can remove local work and recovery copies.

Household organiser, notes and notifications

More → Household organiser holds household information such as shopping, appointments and errands. House notes is for messages kept on the household board. Appointments and eligible errands can appear on Calendar alongside cleaning.

In-app task alerts and external device notifications are separate. Use Settings → Account & sync → Enable external notifications on each device where you want them. Permission and connection status affect delivery. Use Turn off external notifications on that device to disable them.

Phone timer requests are another separate feature; enabling notifications does not set up the phone Clock connection.

If something looks missing or out of date
  • A task is missing: check the selected room, Tasks/Side quests/Recurring tab, search, area filter and completed section. In Doing Now, check whether you selected it. A future or paused recurring task may be in a different recurring status.
  • A recommendation is missing: check Go With the Flow is enabled, Finish setup is complete, the task's recommendation choices/category/priority/date are saved, and it is not As needed, completed or personally hidden. Today's snapshot may not include settings changed until the next refresh.
  • Catch up is missing: this is expected in Go With the Flow. Follow a Routine retains Catch up.
  • Another device differs: check both devices are signed in to the same account and intended house, reconnect, and inspect sync messages. Different members can have different personal modes and recommendations.
  • A phone timer did not appear: check the phone-specific setup, permissions and phone Clock. A submitted request is not confirmation of delivery.
  • The interface looks old after an update: reload the page once your work is saved. Keep using the same site/origin; do not clear saved data or import an older backup to refresh the interface.

If an issue continues, note the page, action, expected result, actual result and any message you saw. Keep your current data intact while the problem is checked.
